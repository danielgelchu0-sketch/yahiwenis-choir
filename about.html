About Us

Our story, mission and vision

Our Story

Yahiwenis Church Choir was founded with a single purpose — to glorify God through the gift of music. What began as a small group of passionate worshippers has grown into a dedicated choir that serves our congregation every week.

Over the years we have performed at church services, community events and special celebrations, always carrying the same spirit of devotion and unity that defined us from the very beginning.

Our Mission

To lead our congregation into meaningful worship through music, to build a community of believers united by faith, and to use our voices as instruments of praise and encouragement.

Our Vision

To be a choir that not only serves within our church walls but reaches out to the wider community — bringing hope, joy and the love of God through every song we sing.

"Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth."
